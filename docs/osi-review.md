# NOSL 1.0 — Open Source Review Notes

## Status

NΞXUS Open Software License (NOSL) 1.0 is currently a release
candidate under development.

It is not OSI-approved.

No OSI approval or endorsement is claimed.

## Purpose

NOSL is designed as a general-purpose software license using a
focused weak-copyleft model.

The intended goal is to allow broad use, modification,
redistribution, and commercial exploitation while requiring
distributed modifications to covered portions of the Software to
remain available under NOSL.

## Intended Freedoms

NOSL is intended to permit:

- use for any purpose;
- study;
- copying;
- modification;
- creation of derivative works;
- redistribution;
- commercial use;
- distribution of modified versions; and
- applicable patent rights granted by Contributors.

## Weak-Copyleft Model

NOSL applies its copyleft requirements to portions of a Covered Work
that are derived from, based upon, or modify material originally
distributed under NOSL.

Independent works are not automatically subject to NOSL merely
because they interact with, link to, communicate with, or
interoperate with NOSL-covered software.

## Distribution

When distributing Covered Work, the distributor must comply with the
source, notice, modification-identification, and license-preservation
requirements defined by the LICENSE.

Commercial distribution is permitted.

## Third-Party Software

Third-party components remain subject to their own applicable
licenses.

NOSL does not attempt to relicense independent third-party
components.

## Patents

NOSL contains a contributor patent grant intended to provide
appropriate patent rights for contributions covered by the license.

The exact legal scope of the patent provisions is subject to legal
review.

## Trademarks

NOSL does not grant trademark rights.

The NΞXUS XS name and branding are separate from copyright
permissions granted by NOSL.

## No Additional Restrictions

NOSL does not permit distributors to add legal or technological
restrictions that prevent recipients from exercising rights granted
by the license.

## No Discrimination

NOSL is intended to permit use by persons and organizations without
restricting use according to identity, nationality, location,
profession, organization, or field of endeavor.

## Relationship to Existing Licenses

NOSL is intended to occupy a weak-copyleft position.

Existing licenses with related goals include the Mozilla Public
License and other copyleft or permissive licenses.

NOSL should be compared against existing OSI-approved licenses
before formal submission to determine whether it provides a
sufficiently distinct and useful licensing option.

## Legal Review

No claim of professional legal review is made by this document.

The license should receive qualified legal review before a formal
submission or final production release.

## Formal OSI Review

Any future OSI submission will be made according to the Open Source
Initiative's current license-review process.

Until an official approval is published by OSI, NOSL must not be
described as an OSI-approved license.
