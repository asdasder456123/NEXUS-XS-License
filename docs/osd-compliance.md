# NOSL 1.0 — OSD Compliance Review

## Status

This document is a pre-submission analysis of NΞXUS Open Software
License (NOSL) 1.0.

It is not an OSI approval decision and does not replace the official
OSI review process.

## OSD 1 — Free Redistribution

NOSL permits redistribution of the Software and does not require a
royalty or fee to NΞXUS XS for exercising the rights granted by the
License.

## OSD 2 — Source Code

NOSL permits distribution in Source Code and Object Code form and
defines Corresponding Source requirements for distributed Covered
Works.

## OSD 3 — Derived Works

NOSL permits modification and derivative works and requires covered
modified portions to remain available under NOSL when distributed.

## OSD 4 — Integrity of The Author's Source Code

NOSL does not prohibit distribution of modified source code.

The License requires material modifications to be identified and
does not require modified source code to retain the original
project's name.

## OSD 5 — No Discrimination Against Persons or Groups

NOSL does not restrict use according to identity, organization,
nationality, location, or other personal or organizational status.

## OSD 6 — No Discrimination Against Fields of Endeavor

NOSL permits commercial and non-commercial use and does not
restrict use according to a field of endeavor.

## OSD 7 — Distribution of License

The rights granted by NOSL apply to recipients of redistributed
Covered Works without requiring recipients to execute a separate
agreement with NΞXUS XS.

## OSD 8 — License Must Not Be Specific to a Product

NOSL is intended as a reusable software license.

It is not limited to a particular NΞXUS XS product or software
distribution.

## OSD 9 — License Must Not Restrict Other Software

NOSL governs only material covered by NOSL.

Its third-party component provisions do not require unrelated
software distributed alongside a Covered Work to adopt NOSL.

## OSD 10 — Technology Neutrality

NOSL does not require a particular technology, platform, interface,
distribution mechanism, or method of user interaction.

## Preliminary Conclusion

This document records the project's current interpretation of the
OSD requirements.

It is not a certification of compliance.

The official OSI review process remains authoritative for any future
OSI approval decision.
