## New-License Requirements

The OSI review process for new licenses requires:

- a description of the gap not filled by existing licenses;
- comparison with the most similar OSI-approved license or licenses;
- disclosure of legal review;
- examples demonstrating potential use beyond the license steward.

These items must be finalized before a formal submission.
